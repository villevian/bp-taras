Remember

Dear Mr / Ms + surname → Yours sincerely · Dear Sir/Madam (no name) → Yours faithfully

enclose = put in the same envelope (letter) · attach = send with an email

Verbs + prepositions (from the letter)

write to sb · confirm sth · talk about sth · pay for sth · deliver to a place · thank sb for sth · look forward to + -ing · apply for · agree on a price

TASK

3A Read the letter. In pairs, discuss how you could improve it.

Hi Ms Liang!
Thanks a lot for the order, we got it. The printers will come to you soon. Like we said, there is a discount. You can pay us later when you get the bill. I put some papers in with this letter. Any problems just call me.
Cheers,
Tom
• Too informal: Hi … ! / Thanks a lot / Cheers → Dear Ms Liang, / We thank you for your business / Yours sincerely
• No order number, product, quantity or price.
• No delivery date or place (soon, to you).
• Discount not specified (8% for orders over 80 units).
• Payment terms unclear (later → 30 days after the date of the invoice).
• some papers → We enclose our terms and conditions for your records.
• Any problems just call me → If you have any queries, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Dear Ms Liang, We are writing to confirm your order number WT3488 for 100 PR765 printers, which we received this morning. The unit price of the printers is €230. I am pleased to confirm that, as we agreed at our meeting last week, there is a discount of 8 percent for any orders over 80 units. We will deliver the goods to your warehouse in Felton Business Park on 5 May. We confirm that our payment terms are 30 days after the date of the invoice. We enclose our business terms and conditions for your records. We thank you for your business and look forward to supplying you again in the future. If you have any queries, please do not hesitate to contact us. Yours sincerely,
