Letter → chat: what changes?
GreetingDear Mr Chahal,Hi Mr Chahal 👋 / Hi Mei
OpeningWe are writing to confirm your order …Quick confirmation of your order #…
Detailslong sentencesshort lines, bullet points, numbers in bold
EnclosuresWe enclose …… attached 📎 / in the thread 🧵
EndingPlease do not hesitate to contact us.Don’t hesitate to message me here.
ClosingYours sincerely,Best regards, / Thanks,

Still professional: full information, correct numbers, no slang, max 1–2 emoji. With a new client — Mr / Ms + surname; with a client you know well — first name.

Verbs + prepositions (from the letter)

write to sb · confirm sth · talk about sth · pay for sth · deliver to a place · thank sb for sth · look forward to + -ing · apply for · agree on a price

TASK

3A Read the message. In pairs, discuss how you could improve it.

# gadget-x-lynx-logistics · Slack Connect
Tom16:05
hey!! got ur order 👍👍 printers coming soon 🚚 discount is ok like we said. pay whenever. sending some docs. ping me if smth lol
• Too informal for a client: hey!!, ur, lol, smth, too many emoji.
• No order number, product, quantity or price.
• coming soon → exact date and place.
• discount is ok → 8% for orders over 80 units.
• pay whenever → payment terms: 30 days after the invoice date.
• some docs → Our T&Cs are attached 📎.
• Ending: Thanks for your business! If anything is unclear, don’t hesitate to message me here.
