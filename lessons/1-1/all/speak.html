Your story – live

2A Tell your 60-second story (Step 9B of Before class) from memory. Don’t read it. Your teacher is a recruiter – she can interrupt you.

🎧 The recruiter may ask: “Sorry – what exactly did you do there?” · “What was the result?” · “Can you give me an example?”

2B Be honest. Which sentences in your story are 100% yours? Did AI help you write it? What was easy and what was hard to say without the text?

💬 2026 truth: using AI is normal. But in an interview there is no AI. If you can’t say it live, it’s not your story yet.
Speaking Soft skills in 2026

3A Discuss the questions with your teacher. Give real examples, not adjectives. Use words from the lesson: audit your experience, bridge the gap, AI-proof, signal initiative, lead by example, through line.

a) If AI writes half your code, what exactly are you paid for?
b) Tell me about a time AI was confidently wrong – and you caught it. What would have happened if you hadn’t?
c) You were a team lead. What was the worst conflict in your team – and what did you do wrong?
d) Is flexibility a real skill – or a nice word for “I can’t say no”?
e) Senior: is it about years or about decisions?
f) In an international team, what’s harder – English, or silence on Zoom and slow replies in Slack?
g) Which soft skill do you not have? What would your old team say about you?
h) Your side project aliasgame.fun: what did real users teach you that your job never did?

3B Which question made you think the most? What is your answer now – in 2–3 sentences?

Role-play An interview at an international company

4A Your teacher is a recruiter. Answer her questions. Use the phrases in the box.

📦 What I actually did was … · The result was … · To be honest, … · I learnt that … · Looking back, I would … · That’s a good question. Let me think …
a) Tell me about yourself.
b) Why should we hire you – and not a junior developer with AI tools?
c) Give an example of a time you influenced a decision without having authority.
d) Tell me about a time you disagreed with your PM. What happened?
e) What’s your biggest weakness – and what are you doing about it?

4B Feedback from your teacher: one thing that worked and one thing to make stronger.
