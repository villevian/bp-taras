Grammar Causative: have something done
have + something + past participle (V3)

We use it when someone else does the job for us (usually a professional, often we pay for it).

I fixed my laptop. → I did it myself.  |  I had my laptop fixed. → A technician did it for me.

PresentWe have our office cleaned every Friday.
PastI had my CV checked by a recruiter.
ContinuousWe’re having our website redesigned.
Future / planI’m going to have my logo redesigned.
Negative / ?We didn’t have the slides translated. · Where do you have your laptop repaired?

get something done = the same, more informal: I need to get my phone fixed.

Compare with the passive: My laptop was repaired. (we only say what happened) — I had my laptop repaired. (I organised it)
